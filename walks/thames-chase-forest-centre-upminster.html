Thames Chase Forest Centre

4.6 Dogs of Essex score

Woodland • Circular • 1hr 10 mins • Mud: Moderate

Upminster Woodland & meadows Mostly off lead Family friendly
Save Email Share

Thames Chase Forest Centre offers a relaxed mix of woodland, meadows and countryside trails, making it a fantastic choice for an everyday dog walk. With accessible paths, varied scenery and plenty of open space, it's particularly popular with families and local dog owners.

Local tip: The main trails are excellent for puppies, older dogs and anyone looking for an easier walk. If you prefer quieter routes, head away from the Forest Centre and explore the wider trail network.
