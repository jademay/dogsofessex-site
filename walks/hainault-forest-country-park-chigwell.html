Hainault Forest Country Park

4.8 Dogs of Essex score

Woodland • Circular • 1hr 15 mins • Mud: Moderate

Chigwell Ancient woodland Mostly off lead Lakeside walks Café nearby
Save Email Share

Hainault Forest combines ancient woodland, open meadows and lakeside trails to create one of the most varied dog walks on the Essex border. With plenty of space to explore and a choice of routes, it's ideal for everything from a quick stroll to a longer adventure.

Local tip: Although Hainault Forest has several lakes, dogs aren't permitted to enter the water. If your dog loves a swim, consider West Mersea Beach instead.
