The Rayleigh Arms

Last checked by Dogs of Essex • June 2026

Overview

A family-run village pub serving fresh, locally sourced food, local real ales and cocktails, with a warm welcome for dogs.

Dog information

Dogs inside Dogs outside

The Rayleigh Arms is dog-friendly, however they have limited dog-friendly tables available. If you are looking to eat, it is advisable to call and book.
